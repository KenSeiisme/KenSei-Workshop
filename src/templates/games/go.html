How to play & rules

Place stones on intersections. Surround an opponent group to capture it. Suicide and prohibited repetition are blocked. Pass when there are no useful moves; both players then review dead stones, seki and the score.

Chinese uses area scoring and positional superko with a published no-result procedure for exceptional cycles. Japanese 1989 uses territory, prisoners, simple ko and mutual final agreement. Special life-and-death confirmation can require adjudication: an unresolved position is never automatically awarded. AGA uses situational superko, pass stones, White last, area scoring and handicap compensation.

Japanese rule examples are still being validated; do not use this app as a tournament referee. Resume play to settle a dispute where the chosen rules permit it.

Chinese standard komi: 7.5 points = 3¾ stones. Japanese: 6.5 points. AGA: 7.5-point tournament preset. Handicap / custom komi games are not ranked.

Online clocks keep running in the background. A continuous 120-second disconnection forfeits unless the clock expires first. Both disconnected: interrupted, with no winner. Closing an online match deliberately counts as resignation.

Local and computer games save privately. Online matches are restored from the server. Keyboard controls apply only while the board is focused. On touch devices, preview a point then confirm; zoom and pan for larger boards.
