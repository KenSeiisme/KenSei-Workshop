How to play & rules

Red moves first on the standard 9 × 10 board. Protect your general; checkmate and having no legal move both lose. Select a piece and destination, drag, or use the board arrow keys and Enter. On touch screens, confirm your preview.

WXF 2018 rules apply. Horse legs, elephant eyes, cannon screens, river and palace restrictions are enforced. Online play has no undo, hints or single-player pause.

This site follows the Chinese original: after the same position returns three times, the responsible player is warned to vary. Repeating that prohibited transition loses. Lawful repetition draws after four returns. This digital referee policy is fixed for rules v1.

The natural move limit is 100 qualifying plies without a capture; pawn moves do not reset it. Only a player's first ten checks count toward that player's verification total. The automatic referee verifies either player's eligible count. Checkmate and repetition forfeits take priority.

Online clocks continue in the background. A continuous 120-second disconnection forfeits unless time expires first. Both disconnected: interrupted. Draw offers keep the clock running.

Private progress saves on this device first; signed-in saves sync to your account. Imported records never become ranked. Fairy-Stockfish runs on this device; its five levels are not official ratings.
