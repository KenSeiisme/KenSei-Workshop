How to play & rules

White moves first. Protect your king; checkmate wins. Use drag and drop or select a piece and destination. On touch screens, confirm your preview. Promotion offers queen, rook, bishop or knight.

Threefold repetition and fifty moves require a claim, including a declared intended move. Fivefold repetition and seventy-five moves end automatically; checkmate takes priority. A draw offer does not stop the clock.

Timeout wins require a legal mating witness. The bounded verifier can leave ordinary middlegames as well as rare dead positions Unresolved; no public win is awarded without proof. Both players may agree to a draw. This is a casual digital game, not a tournament referee.

Online clocks continue in the background. A continuous 120-second disconnection forfeits unless time expires first. Both disconnected: interrupted. Online play has no undo, hints or single-player pause.

Local and computer games save privately. Imported positions are checked for basic legality, not complete historical reachability. Stockfish levels do not represent official ratings.
