A LITTLE TRANSPARENCY

Your work stays yours.

Your language preference is stored on this device. Public site search uses a static index of tools, games and projects and does not search private records.

Browser tools

Image, PDF, ZIP, QR, Markdown, color, unit conversion, text comparison and OCR processing happen in your browser. ZIP contents are checked and extracted locally; archive files are never uploaded. Password Generator uses cryptographic randomness and does not save, log or upload generated passwords or word passphrases. Copying a password places it on your device clipboard. Images, PDF files, QR content, Wi-Fi passwords and logos are not uploaded or saved to your workspace. QR appearance presets contain only colors and size. OCR downloads only the language packs you choose, and caches them in your browser. Existing text tools send text to cloud storage only when you explicitly save a signed-in draft. Answer Book and Decision Studio automatically save new or edited records: locally first, then privately to your signed-in account. Answer Book questions are never saved. Guest history is not automatically uploaded when you sign in.

Send to transfers selected results directly between same-site tabs using temporary memory. Keep the source tab open until receipt. Transfer links expire after five minutes or receipt; files are not uploaded or added to local recovery. Explicitly saved text drafts still use your private workspace.

Settings recipes contain operation parameters, SQL and slot mappings only. Original files, query results, PDF form values, annotation text, signatures and audio never enter recipes. Recipes save only when requested, locally for guests or privately for the signed-in account. Analyze runs in a read-only browser database with external access and extension downloads disabled.

Network Test

Network Test is an online measurement, started only by you. Your browser downloads and uploads synthetic test data directly to Cloudflare, which receives your connection IP and handles requests under its own privacy policy. A Full Test schedules 488.9 MB of payload at most; all attempts including retries share a 500 MB payload budget, with protocol overhead additional. Extra per-measurement and final-result reporting are disabled. The test stops when cancelled, hidden, left or timed out. The last 20 completed results, valid sample curves, connection organization and approximate region are stored only in this browser and can be deleted or cleared; they are never synced to your account. A small Cloudflare /meta request runs once after Start for connection details. Full IP is masked by default, exists only on the current page and is excluded from history, logs and every report. Exported PNG/CSV/JSON reports exclude full IP. Old summary-only history remains readable. Measurements describe your connection to Cloudflare, not every internet destination.

Time, audio and data tools

Time Studio uses your device clock and browser time-zone data. Clock settings and named countdowns stay on this device; it does not provide background alarms. File Renamer downloads renamed ZIP copies without changing originals. Audio files, waveform samples, exported audio, JSON and CSV inputs are processed locally. Audio is never saved or uploaded. JSON and CSV are not uploaded; optional text recovery can retain selected editor text on this device when enabled. MP3 encoding runs locally using a vendored encoder and WASM. CSV downloads escape spreadsheet formula cells and explain any escaping.

Learning cards and local utilities

Learning cards, review mastery and due dates are stored in IndexedDB on this device. Signed-in card decks sync privately to your account; guest decks are not automatically uploaded. Private decks are excluded from public profiles. Conflicts retain copies, and deleted cloud decks keep empty synchronization tombstones. Calculator history stays on this device. File checksums, subtitle files and local preview videos are processed here without uploads. Download subtitle edits before leaving. PDF information cleanup covers the standard Info dictionary and document/page XMP only.

Study plans and whiteboards

Plans and vector whiteboards save first in IndexedDB and sync privately when signed in. Guest records are copied to an account only through Copy to account. Sync queues are isolated by account; conflicting edits retain a copy. Whiteboards do not embed files or external pages. Workspace provides deletion and backups.

Shared whiteboards are separate invitation-only copies, saved with Supabase for up to four signed-in members. Members see the drawing, public profile names, avatars, online presence and live cursors. Shared content is sent to Supabase, unlike local file tools. Only the owner manages invitations and membership. Removing a member changes channel access and invalidates the old invitation. Shared boards remain saved after everyone disconnects; the owner can delete them. Unsaved recovery copies stay on this device, isolated by account. Whiteboard and Pong share a two-active-room limit.

Recording and QR scanning

Screen recording, microphone recording and QR scanning start only after your action and device permission. Camera frames, scanned content and recordings stay on this page, are never uploaded or added to history, and must be downloaded to keep. Stopping or leaving releases capture devices. Screen recording continues when switching windows. Shared audio and recording formats depend on your browser. QR links open only when you choose.

Local workspace

Favorites, presets, saved drafts, timer settings and completed focus sessions are stored in this browser. Clearing browser storage removes local data. Use the workspace export to keep a backup.

Optional account

When configured, Google sign-in is provided through Supabase. Account identity and private items are stored with Supabase, with access limited to your account. Your Google display name and avatar initialize your public profile on first sign-in. Existing chosen names and later profile edits are retained. Profile names, avatars and biographies are public; your email and tool histories stay private. The application requests basic profile and email information, not access to your Google files.

Typing and device tests

Typing Practice scores stay in this browser. Ranked sends your completed input to Supabase to calculate a score; raw typed text is not retained. Public is the default typing mode and requires sign-in; Practice remains local and can be selected without an account. Qualifying best scores publish your current public name, speed, accuracy and date, with a link to your public profile. Uploaded avatars are the only images sent to profile storage, after you explicitly save them; file-tool inputs are never uploaded. Avatars are center-cropped and re-encoded in the browser, then stored publicly in Supabase Storage. Google avatars may be loaded from Google, and can be replaced or removed in Profile. English and Chinese use separate versioned boards. Device tests run only when started, stop when you leave their test area, and never save or upload key or mouse events.

Optional local recovery

Remember on this device is off by default and enabled separately for each supported tool and account. It stores only selected editor text and settings in IndexedDB on this browser, with up to three recent editing sessions and 2 MB per copy. Restore or discard dated copies explicitly. Recovery is separate from Workspace saves and is never automatically synced. Passwords, QR and Wi-Fi content, Answer Book questions, typing input, file objects and media are excluded. Turning recovery off clears this tool’s copies for the current identity. Existing private autosave records keep their original behavior.

Games and public boards

Private game progress and personal bests save on this device. Signed-in account saves sync privately to Supabase, isolated from guest saves. Guest saves copy to an account only when requested. New signed-in games that support rankings automatically submit eligible completed results. Guests can play without signing in; old local results are not uploaded to rankings. Connect Four and Reversi are local-only matches. Their computer opponents run in a browser worker, and local two-player matches use the same device. Public runs send a versioned action replay to Supabase for verification and publish your current profile name, avatar, result and date. Replays are not public and are not retained after verification. Paused, hidden or restored games continue privately and cannot enter the board. Pending completed submissions stay on this device for retry, isolated by account. Leaderboards are casual and cannot prevent automated play. Game saves can be deleted in Games. Sound defaults to 35% and starts only after a game gesture; mute, volume and Effects preferences remain on this device. Brick Breaker current and Previous rules rankings are separate; legacy saves and results are retained. Profile pages include qualifying game bests and unlocked milestone badges with dates. Only server-verified Public results count toward the 30 milestones and personal progress; locked milestone progress is visible only to its owner. Historical badges use only provable results.

Online friend Pong

Pong requires both players to sign in. Room members see each other’s public profile name and avatar. Positions and game controls travel through private Supabase Realtime channels, not per-frame database writes. Only short-lived room lifecycle and membership are stored. Rooms have two players, waiting rooms expire after 15 minutes, and pauses expire after 60 seconds. Pong does not create public rankings, achievements, permanent match records or resumable saves. Leaving, account changes and expired rooms release the connection. Supabase handles connection metadata under its own privacy policy.

Your controls

Delete or clear private histories in Workspace, export each history, or sign out. Autosave caches and retry queues are isolated by account. Conflicting scheme edits are kept as separate copies. Deleted cloud records retain an empty-content tombstone for sync; minimal retry receipts contain no saved record content. Signing out hides account data on this device. The platform does not add advertising trackers.
